## Postscript: Let Everyone Become a Creator

This book set out from an abstract mathematical concept, the dualoid, and tried to give a unified explanation of the evolution of the universe, the composition of matter, the origin of consciousness, the structure of society and the future of human civilization. From the fixed point and minimization-maximization, to tit for tat and the two chain reactions, from the two kinds of resources to the dynamic-weight neural network, from the hash language to the software seed, from personal data sovereignty to the alignment of civilization, and on to the path that begins with one person's cloud storage, this is a continuous evolutionary path from the Big Bang to the singularity of civilization. Every step of the path follows the same underlying law: a process driven by the basic properties of the dualoid, maximizing stable structure through hierarchical emergence. This process is also a process of ever-increasing information, the process of the universe coming to know itself and realize itself.

This is an extremely ambitious attempt, and many of its views may seem radical or even absurd. But as the preface said, when the existing theoretical system has fallen into the trap of a local optimum and cannot resolve its fundamental contradictions, we need a disruptive, counterintuitive sacrifice strategy. What this book proposes is exactly such a sacrifice: give up the binary opposition of object and morphism, give up the traditional practice of excluding paradox from the system, give up the pyramid, give up the computer centered on human natural language, give up the old notion of privacy as defense, and instead embrace a more fundamental single entity that unifies existence and change and contains paradox as the engine of evolution, together with a set of rules built on it that make everyone equal peers.

The evolution of human civilization's institutions is in fact a local instance, on this small planet Earth, of the evolution of the whole universe, and it too follows the emergence mechanism of fixed point plus minimization. The mechanism I envision is nothing more than a civilizational mechanism with higher emergent efficiency.

This theoretical framework is far from complete, let alone rigorously formalized and proven. It is more like the blueprint of a thought experiment, a starting point that invites readers to think together. Building humanity's ultimate theory is a grand undertaking that needs the formal construction of mathematicians, the experimental verification of physicists, the simulated implementation of computer scientists, and the humane concern of philosophers and sociologists. And in the system I envision, these people need not belong to any particular profession. There, everyone is a student and also a teacher; a consumer and also a producer; one of the managed and also a manager; a neuron of this civilization and also its author.

Let everyone become a creator. This is not a slogan. It is what happens naturally once this mechanism is up and running.

Starting with me, one person.
