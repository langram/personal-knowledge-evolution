## I. Core Positioning and Meta-Narrative Architecture

### 1.1 Positioning and Genre Temperament
- **"Self-Fulfilling Science Fiction"**: Directly enacts the vision proposed in Section 12.2 of *Nova Principia*—whereas traditional science fiction merely imagines futures without taking responsibility for realizing them, this novel fuses scientific theory with literary narrative to give humanity a vivid, lived psychological preview of a cooperative post-singularity civilization during the perilous window where technological acceleration vastly outpaces institutional evolution, helping catalyze a non-violent, smooth civilizational transition.
- **An "Anti-*Three-Body* Worldview / Anti-Dark-Forest" Cosmic Manifesto**: If the pinnacle of Chinese science fiction, *The Three-Body Problem* (above all its second volume, *The Dark Forest*), extrapolates Earth's traditional law of the jungle and maximal privacy to interstellar scale, then this novel stands as an explicit **"Anti-*Three-Body* Worldview"** counterweight—replacing the zero-sum abyss of an opaque "universe of privacy" and the "Chain of Suspicion" (a negative chain reaction) with an **"Anti-Dark-Forest Civilization"** built upon zero privacy, transparent public knowledge, and positive cooperative chain reactions.
- **Narrative Form**: First-person ("I"—Lang Tu) philosophical dialogue-driven science fiction in the lineage of Plato's *Republic*, Thomas More's *Utopia*, and *The Man from Earth*. The bulk of the novel unfolds as a marathon overnight conversation and sharp intellectual duel between the protagonist and an undercover alien observer in a southern Chinese coastal city in 2026 following an engineered encounter and one-night stand—transforming the theoretical principles, civilizational blueprints, and modern anxieties of *Nova Principia* into earthy, witty, high-voltage dramatic friction.

### 1.2 Cosmic Backdrop and Two Singularity Intervention Protocols
- From the birth of the cosmos, complexity has continuously emerged and evolved; intelligent life and advanced civilizations are an inevitability, not a fluke. Because the Sun is a relatively young star, advanced civilizations had already crossed the "civilizational singularity" hundreds of millions of years before human civilization arose on Earth.
- Every emerging civilization faces one supreme mortal bottleneck on its path to the singularity: **when the technological singularity leaps far ahead of the institutional and social-structural singularity**, causing the probability of self-destruction to spike dramatically.
- Confronted with an emerging civilization at this threshold, advanced interstellar civilizations apply two protocols:
  1. **Silent Observation (Non-Intervention)**: When the native civilization's institutional evolution keeps pace with its technology and the crossing remains relatively safe and stable, observers strictly refrain from intervening.
  2. **Surgical "Spark Delivery" (Micro-Intervention)**: When institutional evolution stalls far behind technological breakthroughs and self-annihilation becomes perilously likely, an undercover observer identifies a native individual who has already begun forming vague, nascent intuitions close to the new civilizational architecture, engages in direct personal contact to impart the complete framework, and prompts that native to publish **a theoretical treatise paired with a self-fulfilling science fiction novel**—harnessing the twin engines of science and literature to accelerate planetary consensus and help the civilization cross the singularity intact.

### 1.3 Programmatic Recursion Loop (Sci-Fi Version of "Once Upon a Mountain")
- This second protocol—"undercover alien envoy + theoretical treatise + self-fulfilling sci-fi novel"—is deliberately architected as a **self-fulfilling, self-recursive program**:
  - The cosmos's very **first** "Progenitor Singularity Civilization" faced the exact same catastrophic gap between technological acceleration and lagging social institutions.
  - A lone genius scientist in that progenitor civilization first discovered the mathematical and game-theoretic principles of the new civilizational order—and simultaneously invented the medium of "self-fulfilling science fiction," crafting a companion story about an even older advanced alien civilization sending an emissary to deliver the theory and rescue a planet on the brink of self-destruction.
  - Powered by the dual momentum of theoretical treatise plus literary story, the progenitor world embraced the new framework and crossed the singularity smoothly within mere decades. Thereafter, later starfaring civilizations turned that originally fictional plot into a literal interstellar aid protocol, recursing across the cosmos like the classic Chinese folk tale *"Once upon a time there was a mountain, on the mountain stood a temple..."*:
    > **Once upon a time there was a planet, and on that planet an alien told a native the story of crossing the civilizational singularity. And what was the story? The story went: "Once upon a time there was a planet, and on that planet an alien told a native the story of crossing the civilizational singularity..."**

### 1.4 The Dual-Postscript "Rashomon" Prank (Signature Meta-Twist)
- **Novel's Postscript**: The first-person narrator "I" (Lang Tu, doubling as the novel's author) appends a direct link to the theoretical treatise *Nova Principia* and earnestly testifies that his own ideas had originally been vague, half-formed seeds—it was the undercover alien woman who gave him the entire completed theory, architecture, and roadmap.
- **Theoretical Treatise's Postscript (Cross-Book Linkage)**: The real-world author steps forward and candidly tells the literal truth—there was never any alien visitor; writing the companion sci-fi novel was a deliberate "science + literature" strategy designed to attract wider attention, accelerate adoption, and close the deadly gap between runaway technology and stagnant institutions (which doubles as a sly self-reference: since humanity has not yet discovered any other civilization, we ourselves may be that very Progenitor Singularity Civilization, and the author's idea *is* the progenitor scientist's idea!).
- **Reader Experience**: The two postscripts directly contradict yet lock tightly into one another, leaving readers with a mischievous philosophical puzzle: **"Between the two postscripts, which one is actually true?"**

## II. Characters and Setting

### 2.1 Time and Place
- **Time**: Present-day 2026—the eve of the singularity, marked by explosive AI capabilities alongside acute geopolitical and institutional anxiety.
- **Location**: A coastal university city in southern China—blending youthful campus vitality with humid sea breezes, neighborhood gyms, morning dim-sum teahouses, and late-night seaside seafood stalls.

### 2.2 Male Protagonist (First-Person "I") — Lang Tu
- **Profile**: Chinese male in his early forties, Asian.
- **Life Circumstances**: Lifelong bachelor; parents deceased; no spouse or children. Currently unemployed; his savings are modest, yet with zero family overhead and minimalist habits he has enough to live comfortably without selling his time to a boss.
- **Personality & Routine**: Loves strength training, solo travel, and independent reflection. Over years of quiet observation, he has begun incubating vague, unsystematized intuitions about a post-pyramidal civilizational order.
- **Narrative Role**: Never a passive yes-man or straight-man foil. He embodies a sharp, grounded, slightly sardonic 2026 Chinese thinker who challenges the alien heroine head-on over privacy, historical morality, blood feuds, and AI displacement.

### 2.3 Female Protagonist (Undercover Alien Observer) — Qin Jingwei
- **Cover Identity**: Appears to be a Thai-Chinese female graduate student in her early twenties studying at a university in southern China.
- **True Identity**: A citizen of an advanced civilization originating on an Earth-like planet dozens of light-years away. Over **5,000 Earth years old**, she has witnessed nearly the entire recorded evolution of human civilization firsthand.
- **Biological Capability**: Masters advanced **Horizontal Gene Transfer (HGT)**, allowing her to assimilate genetic segments from other individuals to actively reshape her appearance, age, physique, temperament, and even biological sex—blending invisibly among Earthlings across millennia.
- **Mission & Motivation (One Experiences, the Whole Planet Shares)**: Because her homeworld crossed the singularity smoothly via Protocol 1, her people never experienced the legendary "Protocol 2" sci-fi intervention. After joining the galactic network and learning of it, they sent observers to young star systems to wait for an opportunity. With Earth reaching peak singularity danger in 2026, she steps forward to enact and experience Protocol 2 firsthand—and because her civilization shares full memories over mature brain-computer interfaces, **only one envoy needs to live the journey for every citizen on her homeworld to experience every moment, heartbeat, and emotion as their own**.
- **Triple Thematic Layers of Her Name**:
  1. **Comedic Opening Hook**: Teased mercilessly by Lang Tu at their first meal for having "terrible parents who named her after two of history's most reviled figures—Qin Hui and Wang Jingwei—plus a mythical little girl who drowned in the sea."
  2. **Cross-Civilizational Provocation & Relativity Scalpel**: Reveals she deliberately combined Qin Hui's surname and Wang Jingwei's given name as a jarring lens to ignite the novel's centerpiece debate on victor-written history, political trolley problems, the "civilizational black box," and Social Cognitive Relativity.
  3. **Homage to Chinese Civilization**: *"Qin"* marks the dawn of China's unified agrarian empire that she personally witnessed across 5,000 years, while *"Jingwei"* evokes the mythical bird Jingwei filling the vast sea pebble by pebble—bridging the mortal chasm between technological and institutional singularities.

## IV. Chapter-by-Chapter Outline (10 Chapters + Epilogue + Dual Postscripts)

> **Temporal Arc**: The main action compresses into an atmospheric, tightly paced **36-hour span** in a southern Chinese coastal university city in spring 2026 (first afternoon gym encounter -> evening dinner & one-night stand -> dawn reveal -> all-day coastal walk, teahouse, dusk campus, seaside seafood stall -> midnight philosophical climax -> next-morning departure), giving dense Socratic dialogue cinematic rhythm and sensory immediacy.

### Chapter 1 | "Qin Jingwei" at the Gym
- **Setting**: A neighborhood gym and nearby Chaoshan beef noodle shop in a southern Chinese coastal university city, spring 2026.
- **Plot Beats**:
  - Establishes Lang Tu's first-person voice: early-forties bachelor, parents deceased, no family burdens, living simply off modest savings, devoted to lifting weights, traveling, and brooding over humanity's institutional bottlenecks.
  - Meets Qin Jingwei at the squat rack—an athletic Thai-Chinese graduate student in her early twenties with startling poise and wide-ranging intellectual curiosity.
  - **Comedic Hook**: Over steaming beef noodle soup, they exchange names. Lang Tu laughs and teases her relentlessly about her parents' terrible naming sense (invoking both Wang Jingwei and Qin Hui's eternal infamy, plus the drowned mythological girl Jingwei). Unfazed, she smiles mysteriously: *"I chose it myself—and you accidentally guessed half right."*
  - Over homemade plum wine at his ocean-view short-term rental, an effortless, surprisingly intimate one-night stand unfolds.

### Chapter 2 | Dawn Reveal: An Engineered "Close Encounter of the Third Kind"
- **Setting**: Sunlit bedroom and balcony of the coastal rental, early next morning.
- **Plot Beats**:
  - Over morning black tea, Qin Jingwei drops the bombshell: neither the gym meeting nor last night was accidental—it was a carefully orchestrated "Close Encounter of the Third Kind."
  - Reveals her true origin: an observer from an Earth-like planet dozens of light-years away, over **5,000 years old**, witness to nearly all of human recorded history.
  - Explains her twin objectives: (1) contacting a native thinker already incubating the seeds of the new civilizational architecture to deliver the complete blueprint; (2) collecting Lang Tu's DNA via intimacy to leverage **Horizontal Gene Transfer**, reshaping her appearance for her next undercover identity.
  - **Dramatic Tension**: Lang Tu initially suspects an elaborate prank or delusion—until she recites, word for word, unshared institutional theories he had only jotted in offline phone notes or mulled privately in his own head. Shaken yet exhilarated, he agrees to hear her out over breakfast.

### Chapter 3 | Earth Sci-Fi's "Poverty of Imagination"
- **Setting**: A bustling traditional southern dim-sum teahouse down the alley.
- **Plot Beats**:
  - Lang Tu asks the classic Earthling question: if they are so advanced, why not park a giant mothership over Earth and broadcast to eight billion people?
  - **Qin Jingwei's Razor-Sharp Roast of Earth Sci-Fi**: Dissects how human novels, comics, and films merely slap space-age skins onto ancient Earth history:
    1. **Cliché Alien Contact**: Flying saucers, failed diplomatic talks, and violent crackdowns (*The Day the Earth Stood Still*, *Close Encounters of the Third Kind*), or brute-force orbital annihilation (*Independence Day*). Even the clever recent TV series ***Pluribus*** (starring *Better Call Saul*'s Rhea Seehorn), where an alien virus assimilates humanity into a single blissful hive mind, remains a **coercive takeover utterly disregarding individual autonomy and consent**.
    2. **Regressive Political Imaginations**: Interstellar starfaring civilizations still ruled by feudal emperors, dukes, and old republics (*Star Wars*, *Dune*—merely Western medieval and Arab histories in spacesuits), or hyper-polarized corporate oligarchy slums (*Cyberpunk 2077*, *Blade Runner*).
    3. **Primitive Swarm Tropes**: Equating collective intelligence with mindless ants and bees (*Starship Troopers*), unable to conceive of a free, high-individuality dynamic neural civilization.
  - Sets the stage to reveal what a genuinely post-singularity civilization looks like.

### Chapter 4 | Respec-ing Life: Why a 5,000-Year-Old Immortal Never Gets Bored
- **Setting**: A sunny coastal wooden boardwalk lined with blooming bougainvillea.
- **Plot Beats**:
  - Lang Tu voices Earth's classic literary fear of immortality: endless monotony leading to existential exhaustion and a longing for death.
  - **Deconstructing Stagnant Immortality**: Qin Jingwei laughs at Earth's assumption that immortality means staying trapped forever in one static body and static personality.
  - Unveils **"RPG Respec Immortality"** via Horizontal Gene Transfer: exchanging and integrating gene segments to freely alter appearance, age, height, build, temperament, and even undergo a "rebirth" to change biological sex—playing infinite fresh character builds across millennia.
  - Adds human warmth to her character: as an experiential immortal, she genuinely relishes authentic human sensations and minds, turning last night's intimacy from cold sampling into something candid and oddly tender.

### Chapter 5 | Old Friends in Two Minutes: "Nudist Colonies," Brain-Computer Interfaces, and the Zero-Privacy Civilization
- **Setting**: A quiet air-conditioned glass seaside tea lounge with chilled green coconuts.
- **Plot Beats**:
  - Qin Jingwei introduces the cornerstone of her civilization: **privacy has been completely abolished**, and every individual's information forms a searchable public knowledge archive feeding the Large Civilization Model.
  - **Comedic Flashpoint**: Lang Tu freezes and blurts out: *"Wait—does that mean billions of aliens on your homeworld can search and replay what happened in my bedroom last night?!"*
  - **Two Unforgettable Analogies**:
    1. **The Nudist Colony / Naturist Village Effect**: Earthlings recoil from zero privacy only because they live in a clothed, power-asymmetric world where secrets shield against exploitation; once institutional rules eliminate abuse of power, transparency feels as natural and unremarkable as nudity inside a naturist community (echoing *Nova Principia* Sec. 3.2 & 5.2).
    2. **Two-Minute Context Sync Etiquette**: Strangers greet not with handshakes, but by syncing their entire life histories, habits, cognition, and "secrets" via mature BCI in seconds to two minutes—becoming as intimate and frictionless as decades-old friends or an old married couple. She draws the sharp AI parallel: **"The fuller the context you provide an AI agent, the higher its task success rate; human collaboration works identically."**
  - **Bidirectional BCI & Trinity-Style Mastery**: Explains how collective AI wisdom flows back into human brains like Trinity downloading helicopter flight skills in *The Matrix*—while noting the grounded biological caveat that brain capacity remains finite and unused skills still fade, yet reconnecting restores peak fluency instantaneously (echoing Sec. 10.3).

### Chapter 6 | No Governments, No Bosses, No Retirement: Dynamic Neural Societies and Old Regimes in Virtual Games
- **Setting**: A pedestrian overpass at dusk overlooking rush-hour commuters, speeding delivery riders, and glowing office towers.
- **Plot Beats**:
  - Contrasts the exhausted 2026 cityscape below with her civilization: **no nations, governments, corporations, bosses, hierarchies, or authorities**, and no chopped-up "school -> work -> retirement" life stages; every individual lives continuously inside a **dynamic-weight intelligent social neural network** where learning, training, practice, and collective reasoning through the **Large Civilization Model** span an entire lifetime.
  - **Where Did Violence and Conflict Go?**: Eradicated completely from reality—preserved safely inside **immersive virtual RPG games** where citizens can roleplay ancient emperors, capitalists, or medieval wars for entertainment (just as modern Earthlings play violent combat games without taking knives into the street).
  - **Addressing 2026 Earth's Urgent Anxieties**: Lang Tu voices the dread of closed-source AI giants distilling white-collar, blue-collar, and scientific knowledge while laying off humans, plus fears of human cognitive atrophy. Qin Jingwei answers with the Industrial Revolution sports-world-record analogy and outlines Earth's practical low-potential first step: reclaiming personal data sovereignty starting from one person, spare change, open-source cloud storage, Merkle-DAG fair attribution of every knowledge increment, and "external market shell, internal new-order core" peaceful pipeline replacement (foreshadowing *Nova Principia* Ch. 3.2, 4.2, 7, 10.3, 11).

### Chapter 7 | The Fractured Sea and the Thousand-Year Singularity: Why Her Homeworld Skipped Earth's Detours
- **Setting**: A lively outdoor seaside seafood stall by the breakwater at night—chargrilled oysters, claypot congee, cold beer, and sea breeze.
- **Plot Beats**:
  - Lang Tu asks whether her homeworld also nearly blew itself up before an older alien paid them a visit.
  - **Twin Evolutionary Engines of Her Homeworld**:
    1. **Fractured Oceanic Archipelago Geography**: Far smaller land ratio and terrain vastly more fragmented than the Aegean/Mediterranean—no contiguous agricultural plains to fuse with harsh tit-for-tat into massive centralized empires (avoiding China's 2,000-year Qin-to-Qing dynastic loop and Europe's 1,000-year post-Roman stagnation). From Axial Age to singularity took only **1,000 comparable years** instead of Earth's **2,500**.
    2. **Transparent Public History Since the Axial Age**: Every recording technology was devoted to public, multi-party logged, cross-verified historical archives—treating **historical records as the sacred experimental dataset and training corpus of social science**.
  - **True Purpose of Her Visit (One Experiences, All Share)**: Because her world crossed smoothly via Protocol 1, they only learned of the legendary Protocol 2 after joining the interstellar network. Seeing 2026 Earth teetering at the singularity cliff, her world dispatched her as their sole representative—when she finishes, her 5,000 years of memories, perceptions, and emotions will stream losslessly to every citizen of her homeworld.
  - Lang Tu pivots sharply back to the unanswered hook: *"Now tell me the truth about your name—Qin Jingwei."*

### Chapter 8 | The Truth Behind "Qin Jingwei" and the "Historical Black Box" Debate
- **Setting**: Quiet midnight balcony of the coastal rental, dark ocean below, flashing channel beacon in the distance.
- **Plot Beats**:
  - Qin Jingwei reveals both layers of her name: beyond witnessing China's history since the Qin dynasty and "Jingwei filling the sea," she genuinely combined **Qin Hui's surname** and **Wang Jingwei's given name**—noting that in her fractured archipelago world, low-violence pragmatists who avoided total mutual annihilation were celebrated as wise moderates rather than eternal villains.
  - **Lang Tu's Fierce Counterattack**: Representing mainstream Earthlings and Chinese historical conscience, Lang Tu slams his cup down and blasts her alien detachment—pointing out the brutality of Song-era invasions and WWII Japanese fascist atrocities, Qin Hui's frame-ups, and Wang Jingwei's collaboration.
  - **Qin Jingwei's Counter-Strike (The Corrupted Training Set)**:
    1. **Hardline Tit-for-Tat Selection Bias**: Points out that Chinese history was overwhelmingly written by victorious hardliners; even when moderates briefly prevailed (e.g., Qin Hui lauded during peace-faction ascendancy), hardliners soon overturned them and rewrote the record.
    2. **Victor-Distorted History**: Victor-controlled chronicles polish allies into spotless saints and flatten losers into pure one-dimensional demons. Approaching historical truth demands cross-examining **contemporary accounts from all factions** alongside the actors' **own letters, memorials, essays, and poetry**.
    3. **Stark Primary-Source Contrast**: Qin Hui's own memorials and writings were virtually erased over the centuries, leaving only hostile external caricatures; Wang Jingwei lived recently enough that his poetry survived—posing the jarring question of how a supposedly pure cartoon monster could write such fiercely moving verse.
    4. **Political Trolley Problems & The Civilizational Black Box**: When Lang Tu fires back that *"great poetry cannot erase catastrophic betrayal—people change!"*, Qin Jingwei applauds: **precisely because humans are dynamic high-dimensional trajectories, never static 0/1 angels or demons!** Examining Song Gaozong/Qin Hui (halting devastating war to spare peasants and rebuild the economy) vs. Yue Fei's war faction (paying any cost for total reconquest) as opposing reference frames inside a brutal **political trolley problem**, she concludes that Earth's gravest blunder is **smashing its own civilizational black box**—proving why humanity urgently needs an immutable, transparent **Merkle-DAG public historical ledger**.

### Chapter 9 | The "Chemistry" of Blood Feuds, the "Anti-Dark-Forest" Civilization, and the Taiji Yin-Yang
- **Setting**: Four in the morning as the first faint gray-blue of dawn touches the horizon.
- **Plot Beats**:
  - **Startling Historical Evidence of Cross-Camp Empathy**: Qin Jingwei reveals that both Qin Hui and Wang Jingwei actually began as fierce hardliners (Wang Jingwei even risked his life to assassinate the Prince Regent). Yet after prolonged face-to-face communication with their mortal enemies—**Qin Hui with Jin minister Wanyan Chang**, and **Wang Jingwei in prison with Qing statesman Prince Su Shanqi**—breaking information isolation replaced demonization with shared human empathy, turning both toward prioritizing cooperation over annihilation (while noting ancient tragedies stemmed from confining such dialogue to closed elite chambers without universal transparent rules).
  - **Lang Tu's Ultimate Ordinary-Person Challenge**: Voices the heaviest objection held by millions of ordinary people and hardliners: *"What about blood feuds—the murder of a father, the violation of a family, the ruin of a homeland? Can that ever be casually reconciled?"*
  - **Qin Jingwei's Summit Answer — The "Chemical Reaction Theory" of Human Game Dynamics**:
    1. **The Precondition ("It Takes Two Hands to Clap")**: Acknowledges reconciliation required both sides to carry overt or latent biological/cognitive traits of empathy and gentle tit-for-tat—just as chemical bonding requires compatible molecular properties.
    2. **Causal Roots of Blood Feuds**: Most blood feuds escalate from minor disputes between rational actors trapped in emotional hardline tit-for-tat loops; a smaller fraction stems from innate biological deficits combined with violent childhood environments producing antisocial pathology.
    3. **Atomic Electron Shells & Stable Compounds**: Whether an encounter yields peaceful synthesis or explosive carnage (*"a commoner's wrath spills blood five paces; an emperor's wrath litters the land with corpses"*), it is fundamentally action and reaction between divergent cognitive structures—identical to how atomic electron shells and valence electrons govern gentle vs. explosive chemical reactions.
    4. **Engineering Voluntary Cooperative Compounds**: Humanity's goal is not erasing human relativity, but using social science to construct **stable cooperative institutional structures**—where every participant chooses cooperation **freely, voluntarily, and spontaneously after receiving full transparent information**, sharply distinct from external coercion or enslavement (**control vs. counter-control and enslavement vs. counter-enslavement are themselves confrontation, never cooperation**).
  - **Blockbuster Callback to Chapter 3 — Confronting *The Three-Body Problem* and Unveiling the "Anti-Dark-Forest" Civilization**:
    - Turns directly to Chinese sci-fi's towering masterpiece, ***The Three-Body Problem***, and the worldview emblazoned on its second volume: ***The Dark Forest***.
    - Dissects the Dark Forest theory as the **ultimate interstellar extrapolation of Earth's jungle law into an opaque "universe of privacy"**, where the famous **"Chain of Suspicion"** is mathematically identical to the **negative chain reaction** analyzed in *Nova Principia*.
    - Contrasts it head-on with her own **"Anti-Dark-Forest" civilization** (resonating with *Nova Principia* Sec. 3.1, 3.2, and 3.4's 10-light-year interstellar cooperation thought experiment): civilizations that truly survive the singularity dismantle walls of privacy and replace the Chain of Suspicion with transparent public knowledge and positive cooperative chain reactions—otherwise she would have fired a photoid or dual-vector foil instead of spending 5,000 years observing and an entire night delivering a spark.
  - **The AI + Nuclear Weapons Ultimatum & Eastern Philosophical Synthesis**:
    - Poses the haunting 2026 challenge: if even history's most reviled figures could move from mortal hatred toward cooperation once genuine mutual understanding occurred, why can't modern humanity? When **AI and nuclear weapons** place planetary annihilation in human hands, what fate awaits Earth if confrontation remains prioritized over cooperation?
    - Closes on the grand arc from **ancient Chinese Taiji philosophy** (the Yin-Yang fish—Yin embedded within Yang, Yang within Yin, mutually interlocking and transforming) to **Einstein's modern Relativity** to **Social Cognitive Relativity**—guiding humanity out of the Dark Forest, safely across the singularity, into genuine *Datong* (Great Harmony).

### Chapter 10 | A Recursive Farewell: "Once Upon a Planet..."
- **Setting**: Sun-drenched university-town subway entrance under green camphor trees, late morning.
- **Plot Beats**:
  - Before parting, Qin Jingwei delivers the final puzzle piece: the **recursive origin tale** of the Progenitor Singularity Civilization's lone genius scientist who invented the "theoretical treatise + self-fulfilling sci-fi novel" method, turning fiction into cosmic protocol ("Once upon a time there was a planet...").
  - Bids Lang Tu a breezy, everyday goodbye: *"My thesis defense passed—I've graduated. Flying back to Thailand tomorrow morning."* Both know silently that tomorrow "Qin Jingwei" will dissolve into thin air as she integrates his genetic sample via horizontal gene transfer and steps into a fresh face elsewhere.
  - Watching her blend effortlessly into the vibrant 2026 morning crowd, Lang Tu finally feels the full quiet weight of the three characters *Qin Jingwei*.
  - Closing image: back in his ocean-view rental, Lang Tu opens his laptop to a blinking cursor on a blank page and begins writing—both the rigorous theoretical treatise *Nova Principia* and this companion self-fulfilling science fiction novel.

## V. Dual-Postscript "Rashomon" Linkage Design

### 5.1 This Novel's Postscript (Narrator: First-Person Protagonist Lang Tu)
- Written in a candid, restrained, quietly awed voice, linking directly to the companion theoretical treatise *Nova Principia: Mathematical Principles of Social Science*.
- Testifies that his own pre-2026 intuitions were vague and fragmentary—it was Qin Jingwei who gave him the completed theory, system architecture, and peaceful evolutionary roadmap.
- **Explicit Positioning Statement**: Declares this book an **"Anti-*Three-Body* Worldview / Anti-Dark-Forest" self-fulfilling science fiction novel**—offering a rigorous, hopeful cosmic alternative to the opaque privacy universe and Chain of Suspicion.

### 5.2 Companion Theoretical Treatise's Postscript (Narrator: The Real-World Author)
- Candidly confesses in the closing note of *Nova Principia* that no 5,000-year-old alien graduate student ever visited—writing the companion sci-fi novel was a deliberate "science + literature" two-track experiment to broaden public imagination, accelerate implementation, and close the perilous gap between technological acceleration and institutional stagnation.
- **Formal Positioning of the Novel**: Explicitly introduces the companion novel as an **"Anti-*Three-Body* Worldview (Anti-Dark-Forest)" self-fulfilling science fiction work**, pinpointing the Dark Forest as an interstellar extrapolation of jungle law in a privacy-bound universe and the Chain of Suspicion as the negative chain reaction—contrasting it with the transparent, cooperative "Anti-Dark-Forest" civilizational model built across both books.
- Leaves a sly, lingering self-referential wink: since humanity has yet to detect any extraterrestrial civilization, perhaps Earth itself is that very **Progenitor Singularity Civilization**—and the author's invention is the progenitor scientist's own strategy come to life.

## VI. Open Creative Backlog (Interfaces for Future Expansion)
