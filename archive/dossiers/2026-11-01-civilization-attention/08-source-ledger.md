# 本轮外部依据与来源边界

新增正文中的研究与事实引用如下。社会架构、文明奇点和元规则是本书提出的研究方向，不能据引用推定已被这些研究验证。已有章节的历史引用原样保留，本轮未重新核查全部历史事实。

|来源|材料类型|可支持的范围与限制|
|---|---|---|
|[Competitive brain activity in visual attention](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9142748/)|research review|Visual selection and competition; does not establish a literal QKV implementation in brains.|
|[Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/html/1706.03762v7)|research|QKV, attention and parallelization; social functions and authority boundaries are this book's design proposals.|
|[Temporal Graph Networks for Deep Learning on Dynamic Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.10637)|research|Event-based changing graph representations, not a complete model of civilization.|
|[Beyond Markets and States: Polycentric Governance of Complex Economic Systems](https://web.pdx.edu/~nwallace/EHP/OstromPolyGov.pdf)|research|Multiple centers and scales of governance, not proof of the proposed attention architecture.|
|[Normal-form games and Nash equilibrium](https://www.mit.edu/~6.7980/nfgs_nash.html)|university teaching|Definition and examples of equilibrium; no implication of fairness or universal learning convergence.|
|[Mechanism Design Basics](https://timroughgarden.org/f13/l/l2.pdf)|university teaching|Incentives and truthful reporting under specified mechanisms; not a general society-wide guarantee.|
|[Algorithmic Game Theory](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~eva/algorithmic-game-theory.pdf)|research textbook|Cooperative games and cost sharing; contribution scores still require a defensible characteristic function and counterfactual assumptions.|
|[A Simple Adaptive Procedure Leading to Correlated Equilibrium](https://www.ma.huji.ac.il/hart/papers/adapt.pdf)|research|Regret matching and empirical correlated equilibrium in fixed finite games; no convergence claim for an open-ended civilization.|
|[Markov Games as a Framework for Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning](https://mlanthology.org/icml/1994/littman1994icml-markov/)|research|Markov-game framework; this paper's demonstrated algorithm emphasizes two-player zero-sum games. General formulations are discussed in the accompanying stochastic-games teaching source.|
|[Stochastic games](https://www.mit.edu/~6.7980/stochastic_games.html)|university teaching|States, histories and strategies; state sufficiency remains a modeling assumption.|
|[Emergent Tool Use From Multi-Agent Autocurricula](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.07528)|research|Emergent tool-use behavior within an engineered environment; not proof that arbitrary future invention can be predicted.|
|[Enhanced POET](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/wang20l.html)|research|Coevolution of challenges and agents in bounded representations; no unlimited open-endedness guarantee.|
|[After Watchmen: It's Summer and We're Running Out of Ice](https://www.dc.com/blog/2019/10/27/after-watchmen-its-summer-and-were-running-out-of-ice)|publisher commentary|Original comic's fabricated alien squid attack; publisher search extract inspected, direct page access returned 403.|
|[Watchmen (2009 film), plot and adaptation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Watchmen_%282009_film%29)|secondary film reference|Film frames Doctor Manhattan; plot checked, with no use as evidence about actual states or necessary outcomes.|
|[AlphaEvolve research account, 14 May 2025](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphaevolve-a-gemini-powered-coding-agent-for-designing-advanced-algorithms/)|research-team account|Verified program search used in algorithms and computing/AI infrastructure; no demonstrated unrestricted recursive general intelligence or singularity date.|
|[Darwin Gödel Machine research account, 30 May 2025](https://sakana.ai/dgm/)|research-team account|Agent code self-modification with evaluation in programming tasks; limited benchmarks and evaluation integrity remain relevant.|
|[US AI Action Plan announcement, 23 July 2025](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2025/07/white-house-unveils-americas-ai-action-plan/)|official policy statement|Published competition-oriented policy goals; not evidence of all motivations or an irreversible race.|
|[Global AI Governance Action Plan, 26 July 2025](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/eng/xw/zyxw/202507/t20250729_11679232.html)|official policy statement|Published cooperation/governance proposals; publication does not establish their implementation or effectiveness.|
|[Strategy-proofness and Arrow's conditions, Satterthwaite (1975)](https://www.kellogg.northwestern.edu/academics-research/research/detail/1975/strategy-proofness-and-arrows-conditions-existence-and-correspondence-theorems/)|research|Conditional social-choice impossibility result, not an unconditional claim that all democratic mechanisms are impossible.|
